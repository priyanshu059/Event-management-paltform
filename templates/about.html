About EventOps AI

EventOps AI transforms traditional event management into an intelligent, AI-powered experience by simplifying planning, registration, coordination, analytics, and communication.

Who We Are

The team behind autonomous event intelligence

EventOps AI is an AI-powered event management platform designed to help organizations, colleges, companies, and communities efficiently organize events from start to finish.

The platform automates event registration, attendee management, scheduling, venue coordination, speaker management, notifications, analytics, and reporting using Artificial Intelligence.

Our goal is to reduce manual effort while improving event quality, decision making, and attendee experience through intelligent automation.
